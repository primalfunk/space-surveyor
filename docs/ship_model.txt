SHIP UPGRADE VISUALIZATION SPEC (V0)

Base:
- Ship uses a fixed top-down sprite silhouette.
- All upgrades are represented as interior lighting or line overlays.
- No geometry changes per upgrade.

UPGRADES

1. SHIELD
- Internal: single light node near power core.
- Light color encodes max shield tier.
- Light brightness encodes current shield HP.
- External: circular aura around ship.
- Aura invisible at level 0.
- Aura color matches internal light.
- Aura opacity proportional to current HP.

2. FIRE RATE
- Interior region: forward emitter chamber.
- Visual: radial ticks/bars.
- Number of ticks = fire rate level.
- Ticks pulse continuously at firing cadence.
- Animation runs even when not firing.

3. FIRE DISTANCE
- Interior region: central forward axis.
- Visual: thin axial line extending forward.
- Length increases with level.
- Higher levels add segmentation or secondary ghost line.
- No pulsing; steady presence.

4. FUEL TANK
- Interior region: aft central chamber.
- Visual: vertical fill bar or stacked cells.
- Max height scales with fuel tank level.
- Fill amount reflects current fuel.
- Depletes during thrust, refills when idle.

5. COLLECTOR
- Interior region: left/right lateral channels.
- Visual: inward-facing chevrons.
- Number of chevrons = collector level.
- Higher levels animate chevrons inward.
- Optional faint external pickup radius at high levels.

GENERAL
- All indicators use stroke + alpha, minimal fill.
- Colors are muted at low levels, clearer at high levels.
- Indicators should be readable at small on-screen size.
