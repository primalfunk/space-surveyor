Discovery/intro alerts and their firing conditions

Systems online. Survey and exit freely.
Trigger: intro.enabled and intro.clock >= INTRO.START_DELAY and intro.flags.systems is false.

Lore points reveal clues and increase score.
Trigger: intro.controlUsed and intro.flags.goals is false. (Highlights goal/exit)

Momentum matters. Chains and distance amplify score.
Trigger: (intro.firstSurveyComplete OR intro.clock >= INTRO.SCORE_TIMEOUT) and intro.flags.score is false. (Highlights score)

Fuel is freedom. Drift wisely.
Trigger: ship.maxFuel > 0, fuel ratio <= INTRO.FUEL_RATIO, and intro.flags.fuel is false. (Highlights fuel)

Space is not uniform. Patterns emerge further out.
Trigger: (intro.sectorTransitions >= 1 OR ring >= 1) and intro.flags.weird is false. (Highlights vignette)

Currents shape motion. Ride them.
Trigger: getClosestRiverInfo(...) returns a river with dist < width/2 and intro.flags.rivers is false. (Highlights river)

Stars bend paths. Respect their pull.
Trigger: active stars exist, computeStarAccelAt magnitude >= INTRO.STAR_PULL_ACCEL, and intro.flags.stars is false.

Distance is remembered.
Trigger: intro.sectorTransitions >= INTRO.LONGRUN_TRANSITIONS and intro.flags.distance is false. (Highlights score; releases suppressed alerts afterward)

Not everything here is inert. (Same text, different triggers)
Anomaly sector: sector.sectorType === SECTOR_TYPES.ANOMALY
Echo sector: sector.sectorType === SECTOR_TYPES.ECHO
Moving stars: any star.motion
Station present: sector.station
