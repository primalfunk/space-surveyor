OCCLUSION & IMPEDANCE SECTOR MODIFIERS SPEC (v1)
Visual Obscuring and Motion Drag as Primary Weirdness Axes

This document defines two concrete, implementable sector modifiers:
VISUAL OCCLUSION and MOTION IMPEDANCE.

These modifiers are additive overlays on top of existing sector templates
(normal sectors and archetype sectors such as Apse, Meridian, Palimpsest,
Quiet Reach).

They are designed to increase perceived weirdness through perception and effort,
not danger or failure.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN INTENT (LOCKED)
----------------------------------------------------------------

1) Weirdness should feel physical, not abstract.
2) Cost should be time, fuel, and attention — never sudden failure.
3) Players should sense increasing resistance from the universe, not hostility.
4) Effects must be legible, tunable, and cheap to implement.
5) These modifiers may scale continuously with progression.

----------------------------------------------------------------
MODIFIER AXIS A — VISUAL OCCLUSION
----------------------------------------------------------------

Concept:
Reduction of long-range visibility through space fog or interference.
Represents uncertainty, loss of clarity, and degraded perception.

Occlusion NEVER:
- damages the player
- hides immediate collision geometry without warning
- removes the scan point entirely

----------------------------------------------------------------
A1. FULL OCCLUDED FIELD
----------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
A uniform fog layer fills the sector, limiting visibility to a radius
around the player ship.

Visual behavior:
- Distant objects fade smoothly into fog
- Near-field objects remain fully visible
- Fog density increases gradually with distance

Implementation notes:
- Radial alpha mask centered on player
- OR depth-based fade (distance from camera)
- No true volumetric simulation required

Tuning parameters:
- visibility_radius
- edge_softness
- fog_opacity
- scan_point_visibility_override (boolean)

Player experience:
- Encourages slower movement
- Forces reliance on near-field awareness
- Increases tension without threat

Recommended usage:
- Palimpsest sectors
- Mid to late progression normal sectors

----------------------------------------------------------------
A2. PATCHY OCCLUDED FIELD
----------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
Fog exists in discrete regions rather than filling the entire sector.

Visual behavior:
- Fog blobs or sheets obscure portions of space
- Visibility clears immediately upon exiting a fog region

Implementation notes:
- Multiple fog volumes with circular or irregular masks
- No collision required

Tuning parameters:
- number_of_fog_patches
- patch_radius_range
- patch_density_variance

Player experience:
- Encourages path selection
- Creates emergent routes and hiding spaces
- Fog becomes navigational terrain

Recommended usage:
- Normal sectors
- Palimpsest sectors
- Rarely in Meridian sectors (early only)

----------------------------------------------------------------
MODIFIER AXIS B — MOTION IMPEDANCE
----------------------------------------------------------------

Concept:
Regions of space apply drag to the player ship, increasing effort and fuel cost.
Represents fatigue, resistance, and the cost of continued motion.

Impedance NEVER:
- immobilizes the ship
- overrides player input
- causes damage

----------------------------------------------------------------
B1. DRAG FIELD
----------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
Entering a drag field applies linear damping to the ship’s velocity.

Mechanical behavior:
- Translation speed reduced
- Thrust remains responsive but less effective
- Fuel consumption increases proportionally

Implementation notes:
- Apply velocity *= drag_factor per update
- Optional fuel_burn_multiplier while inside field
- Rotation may be unaffected (recommended default)

Tuning parameters:
- drag_factor
- fuel_multiplier
- affects_rotation (boolean)

Player experience:
- Movement feels “thick” or “heavy”
- Pushing through is possible but costly
- Encourages intentional movement

Recommended usage:
- Mid to late game sectors
- Near Quiet Reach
- As escalation of normal space

----------------------------------------------------------------
B2. LAYERED DRAG FIELDS
----------------------------------------------------------------

Description:
Multiple drag regions with varying strength exist in a single sector.

Mechanical behavior:
- Some regions mildly impede motion
- Others significantly slow progress
- Visual density correlates imperfectly with drag strength

Implementation notes:
- Same drag logic as B1
- Different parameters per region instance

Tuning parameters:
- min_drag_factor
- max_drag_factor
- correlation_to_visual_density (loose, not exact)

Player experience:
- Players learn not to trust appearance fully
- Careful probing rewarded
- Reinforces interpretive uncertainty

Recommended usage:
- Palimpsest sectors
- Late-game normal sectors

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMBINED MODIFIERS (RECOMMENDED PATTERNS)
----------------------------------------------------------------

C1. OCCLUDED + DRAG (HEAVY SILENCE)
- Full or patchy fog combined with drag fields
- Low visibility + high effort
- Strong late-game withdrawal tone

C2. CLEAR BUT HEAVY
- No visual occlusion
- Global low-level drag applied everywhere
- Feels subtly wrong despite clarity

C3. FOG WITH CHANNELS
- Dense fog + drag in most of sector
- Clear, low-drag corridors cut through
- Encourages following invisible “currents”

----------------------------------------------------------------
PROGRESSION SCALING GUIDELINES
----------------------------------------------------------------

Early game:
- Light occlusion only
- Minimal or no drag
- Effects mostly atmospheric

Mid game:
- Patchy occlusion
- Noticeable drag regions
- Fuel tradeoffs become meaningful

Late game:
- Dense occlusion
- High drag fields
- Clear-but-heavy sectors introduced
- Quiet Reach adjacency increases

----------------------------------------------------------------
COMPATIBILITY RULES
----------------------------------------------------------------

- Occlusion and Drag may stack.
- Quiet Reach sectors may use Drag but should never use Occlusion.
- Meridian sectors should use Occlusion sparingly and Drag minimally.
- Apse interiors may include localized Drag for difficulty tuning.
- Palimpsest sectors freely combine both axes.

----------------------------------------------------------------
NON-NEGOTIABLE DESIGN AXIOMS
----------------------------------------------------------------

1) No sudden failure states.
2) No surprise damage.
3) No instant loss of control.
4) The universe resists; it does not attack.
5) Effort replaces danger.

END OF SPEC
