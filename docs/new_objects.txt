FEATURE ADDITIONS — OBJECTS, SPAWNS, DROPPABLES

OBJECT TYPES

1) CORE
- Behavior:
  - On destruction, triggers an explosion with radius = 3x standard explosion radius.
- Drops:
  - None (for now).

2) LURE
- Spawn rules:
  - 5% chance per sector.
  - Never spawns in special sector types.
- Behavior:
  - Passive until shot.
  - When shot, immediately spawns 1–3 enemy ships into the current sector
  - Initial placement of the ships is just beyond player's visual range and, if multiple, scattered wide
- Drops:
  - None.

3) WRECKAGE
- Spawn rules:
  - 10% chance per sector.
- Behavior:
  - Static / destructible.
- Drops:
  - relic_droppable.png

4) NODE
- Spawn rules:
  - Normal sector rules (non-hostile entity).
- Behavior:
  - Mobile, semi-intelligent.
  - Approaches the player ship.
  - Follows / observes the player for a short duration.
  - Loses interest by default and departs the sector.
- On destruction:
  - Drops node_droppable.png

5) SHARD
- Spawn rules:
  - 20% chance per sector.
  - Cannot spawn in special sectors.
  - Start sector is allowed.
- Behavior:
  - Static / destructible.
- Drops:
  - shard_droppable.png


DROPPABLES (INSTANT UPGRADES)

All droppables apply their effect immediately on pickup.
Effects are permanent and equivalent to shop-purchased upgrades.

1) relic_droppable
- Effect:
  - Increase player hull by +1.

2) shard_droppable
- Effect:
  - Increase player fire rate.

3) node_droppable
- Effect:
  - Increase player fuel tank capacity.


GLOBAL NOTES

- Special sector types never contain Lures or Shards.
- Lures and Cores are encounter-modifying objects and do not provide direct player upgrades.
- Droppables are single-effect, instant-use, non-inventory items.
